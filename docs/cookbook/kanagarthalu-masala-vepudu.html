Kanagarthalu Masala Vepudu (Andhra Mackerel Fry)కనగర్తలు మసాలా వేపుడు

Kanagarthalu (mackerel) is the oily, deeply savoury fish of the Andhra coast. Here it gets a hot, tangy ginger-garlic masala, a quick rest, and a pan-sear in coconut oil until the crust turns dark red and crisp, with curry leaves fried right alongside. No flour coating, no rice flour, nothing but spice.

5.2g net carbs  ·  48g protein  ·  48g fat  ·  660 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 67% ● Protein 30% ● Net Carbs 3%

Ingredients
  • mackerel fillets or steaks (fresh or thawed), skin on, cut into 5 cm pieces 2 large fillets · 500g
  • lemon juice, for the marinade 1 tbsp · 15g
  • ginger, grated 2 tsp · 8g
  • garlic, crushed to a paste 2 to 3 cloves · 8g
  • Guntur red chilli powder 2 tsp · 5g
  • turmeric powder 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • coriander powder 1 1/2 tsp · 3g
  • garam masala 3/4 tsp · 2g
  • black pepper, freshly ground 1/2 tsp · 1g
  • fennel seeds, lightly crushed 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • coconut oil for pan-frying 2 tbsp · 25g
  • fresh curry leaves about 25 leaves · 5g
  • onion, sliced paper-thin, to serve raw a few rings · 15g
  • salt to taste · 4g
Method
  1. Pat the mackerel completely dry with kitchen paper (wet fish steams instead of searing) and make 2 or 3 shallow slits in the skin. Rub with the salt and half the lemon juice and leave for 5 minutes; this also tames the fishy smell.
  2. Mix the ginger, garlic, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, pepper, fennel and the rest of the lemon juice with 1 tbsp water into a thick paste, adding drops more water only if it will not spread.
  3. Rub the paste over every surface and into the slits. Rest 15 minutes at room temperature (up to 30 minutes), or refrigerate for up to 3 hours.
  4. Heat the coconut oil in a cast-iron or heavy non-stick pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Lay the fish in skin-side down, away from you, and fry 3 to 4 minutes without moving it, until the underside is deep red-brown and releases easily.
  5. Flip carefully, scatter the curry leaves into the oil around the fish and fry 2 to 3 minutes until the leaves are crisp and the second side is browned and the thickest piece flakes at the centre.
  6. Rest on kitchen paper 1 minute, pile the raw onion rings on top and serve at once with lemon wedges; cauliflower rice with curry-leaf talimpu is the classic keto partner.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Kanagarthalu (Indian mackerel / bangda): Atlantic or Spanish mackerel fillets; or king mackerel steaks; sardines or tilapia fillets also work (shorten cook time)
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: 99 Ranch, Mitsuwa (San Jose), Lunardi's or Whole Foods fish counter; frozen bangda at Patel Brothers and New India Bazaar
  • Fresh curry leaves: Frozen curry leaves straight from the freezer
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara), New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale)
  • Guntur red chilli powder: Hot Indian chilli powder, or cayenne with a little Kashmiri chilli powder for colour
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers, New India Bazaar
  • Coconut oil: Refined coconut oil for a neutral flavour; avocado oil if you want no coconut note
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Trader Joe's, Costco, Sprouts
Make it taste like home
  • Mackerel is rich and cooks fast; the dark-red crust is the target, so leave it alone for the full 3 to 4 minutes before flipping.
  • No flour, no rice-flour crust: the thin masala paste is what crisps, which keeps the net carbs at the spice and aromatic level only.
  • Keto count: the lemon, garlic and ginger together add about 4 g of carbs across two servings, so keep them measured.
  • Make-ahead: the marinated fish keeps 3 hours in the fridge; cook it only when you are ready to eat.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • For heat 2 to 3, use 2 tsp chilli powder and add 1/2 tsp extra pepper for warmth.
  • If mackerel is too strong for you, use tilapia or catfish fillets with the same marinade, 2 minutes less on each side.
