Chicken 65 (Air-Fryer or Shallow-Fry, Almond-Flour Coating)చికెన్ 65

Crisp-edged, brick-red cubes of thigh with a garlicky curry-leaf and green-chilli tempering. The coating is yogurt, egg and almond flour: no maida, no cornflour, no sugar.

  • 7.3 g net carbs
  • 45 g protein
  • 27 g fat
  • 466 kcal
  • 28 min
  • Serves 2
Fat 54%Protein 40%Net carbs 6%per serving
Ingredients
  • boneless skinless chicken thigh, cut into 2.5 cm cubesabout 3 to 4 thighs · 400 g
  • plain whole-milk yogurt (curd)3 tbsp · 40 g
  • egg, lightly beaten1/2 large egg · 25 g
  • almond flour (plain, finely ground, no added starch)3 tbsp · 20 g
  • ginger, crushed1 inch piece · 8 g
  • garlic, crushed (marinade)3 cloves · 10 g
  • Kashmiri or Guntur red chilli powder (marinade)2 tsp · 5 g
  • turmeric powder1/4 tsp · 1 g
  • coriander seed, ground3/4 tsp · 1.5 g
  • cumin seed, ground1/3 tsp · 0.7 g
  • cinnamon and cloves, ground (garam masala base)a pinch of each · 0.8 g
  • black pepper, ground1/2 tsp · 1 g
  • lemon juice (marinade)2 tsp · 10 g
  • fresh curry leaves, finely chopped (marinade)1/2 sprig · 2 g
  • salt3/4 tsp · 3 g
  • avocado oil, for the air fryer or shallow fry1 tbsp · 15 g
  • sesame oil, for the tempering1/2 tbsp · 7 g
  • garlic, chopped (tempering)2 cloves · 6 g
  • green chillies, slit (tempering)2 · 10 g
  • fresh curry leaves, whole (tempering)1 sprig · 3 g
  • red chilli powder (tempering paste)3/4 tsp · 2 g
  • lemon juice (finish)1 tsp · 5 g
  • coriander leaves, choppeda small pinch · 3 g
Method
  1. In a bowl, mix the yogurt, egg, ginger, 10 g crushed garlic, 5 g chilli powder, turmeric, ground coriander, ground cumin, the cinnamon-clove pinch, pepper, lemon juice, chopped curry leaves and 2 g of the salt into a loose paste. Add the chicken cubes and mix well. Sprinkle the almond flour over and fold until every cube has a thick, clinging coat. Rest 10 minutes (longer in the fridge is better, see tips). Preheat the air fryer to 190 C / 375 F.
  2. While the chicken rests, mix the tempering paste: 2 g chilli powder, the last 1 g of salt, and 2 tbsp water in a small bowl. Chop 6 g garlic and slit the green chillies.
  3. Air-fryer route: brush the basket lightly with 1/2 tbsp avocado oil, spread the cubes in one layer without crowding (cook in two batches if needed), and drizzle or spray the remaining oil over the tops. Air fry 8 minutes, flip, then 3 to 4 more minutes until deep golden and a cube reads 74 C / 165 F inside. For the last 2 minutes add a sprig of curry leaves and the slit chillies to the basket.
  4. Shallow-fry route: heat all 15 g avocado oil in a wide nonstick or cast-iron pan on medium-high until it shimmers. Lay in the cubes in one layer, leaving gaps. Cook 3 to 4 minutes undisturbed until the underside is golden and releases easily, flip, and cook 3 to 4 minutes more; work in batches so they fry instead of steam, until a cube reads 74 C / 165 F inside.
  5. Heat the sesame oil in a wok or kadai over medium-high. Add the chopped garlic, the remaining curry leaves and the slit green chillies and stir-fry 20 to 30 seconds until the curry leaves turn crisp (do not brown the garlic). Lower the heat, add the chilli paste and cook 10 seconds until it smells fragrant.
  6. Add the hot chicken, toss on high heat for 30 to 45 seconds until every piece is glossy and red, then finish with the lemon juice and coriander. Serve immediately so the crust stays crisp.
Make it taste like home
  • Taste tip: the garlic-curry-leaf-chilli tempering in step 5 is what turns this from 'air-fried chicken' into Chicken 65. Do not skip it.
  • Keto tip: no sugar, no cornflour, no maida. The coating's carbs are about 0.9 g net carbs per serving from the almond flour. Check that your almond flour is plain and finely ground with no added starch.
  • Make-ahead: marinate with the yogurt, egg and spices in the morning (up to 12 hours, covered, in the refrigerator); fold in the almond flour just before cooking so it does not turn pasty. Leftovers re-crisp in the air fryer at 190 C for 3 minutes.
  • Weekday note: boneless thigh in small cubes cooks in 12 minutes in the air fryer, far faster than bone-in.
Swaps
  • Heat 3: use Kashmiri chilli powder for both the marinade and the paste, and drop one green chilli.
  • No air fryer: use the shallow-fry route in step 4, or bake at 220 C for 18 minutes on a rack.
