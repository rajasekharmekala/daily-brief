Kobbari Karam (Garlic Coconut Chilli Powder)కొబ్బరి కారం

Toasted dry coconut ground with fiery chillies, cumin and garlic: a crumbly, nutty karam that turns hot cauliflower rice or a fried egg into something you would walk across town for. One serving is a heaped tablespoon.

1.1g net carbs  ·  1g protein  ·  3g fat  ·  38 kcal  ·  Serves 12

Ingredients
  • unsweetened dried coconut (endu kobbari), grated or desiccated about 1 cup · 60g
  • dried red chillies 5 to 6 chillies · 8g
  • garlic cloves, peeled 6 medium cloves · 15g
  • cumin seeds 1½ tsp · 4g
  • salt ¾ tsp · 4g
Method
  1. Toast the coconut in a dry heavy pan on low for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring constantly, until it turns pale gold and smells nutty. Tip it onto a plate to cool.
  2. In the same pan, dry-roast the peeled garlic cloves on low for about 3 minutes until freckled brown and dry on the surface. Remove. Roast the chillies and cumin for about a minute until the chillies darken and crisp.
  3. Let everything cool completely, at least 10 minutes. Warm ingredients turn the coconut oily and the powder clumps.
  4. Grind the chillies, cumin and salt first to a coarse powder. Add the coconut and garlic and pulse 5 or 6 times in short bursts until it looks like crumbly damp sand. Do not run the grinder continuously.
  5. Cool once more, then pack into a clean, dry airtight jar. Serve a heaped tablespoon over hot cauliflower rice with a teaspoon of ghee.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh Grated Coconut (Kobbari): Frozen grated coconut (Daily Delight / Vadilal from Indian grocers) or unsweetened desiccated coconut rehydrated with 2 tbsp warm water or coconut cream. Coconut milk/cream from Trader Joe's or Whole Foods (unsweetened) works seamlessly in gravies.
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Trader Joe's (unsweetened coconut flakes/milk), Patel Brothers, Safeway.
Make it taste like home
  • Storage: the raw-garlic style tastes best in the first 5 days and keeps at least 10 days at room temperature (MySpicyKitchen); the fridge extends that. Leave the garlic out for a pantry version that keeps for weeks. Always use a dry spoon.
  • Keto note: with no dal, one tablespoon is only about 1 g net carbs. Use unsweetened coconut; sweetened desiccated coconut can add several grams of sugar.
  • Use it over cauliflower rice, scrambled or fried eggs, sautéed greens, or stirred into plain yoghurt as a dip.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Lower heat: use 3 chillies, or swap part of them for Kashmiri chillies for colour without as much fire.
  • Classic version: add 1 tbsp each of roasted chana dal and urad dal; it tastes more traditional but adds a few grams of carbs.
