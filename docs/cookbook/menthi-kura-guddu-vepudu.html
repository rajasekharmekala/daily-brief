Menthi Kura Guddu Vepudu (Fenugreek-Leaf Egg Fry)మెంతికూర గుడ్డు వేపుడు

Fresh fenugreek leaves are wilted hard in a garlicky talimpu so the bitterness turns into a savoury edge, then four eggs are set in the greens until they cling in golden, spicy curds. Fifteen minutes, one pan, and nothing like a plain scramble.

  • 6.0 g net carbs
  • 15 g protein
  • 17 g fat
  • 244 kcal
  • 15 min
  • Serves 2
Fat 65%Protein 25%Net carbs 10%per serving
Ingredients
  • fresh fenugreek leaves (menthi kura), picked and finely choppedabout 2 packed cups · 80 g
  • eggs, shelled4 large · 200 g
  • ghee1 tbsp · 14 g
  • cumin seeds1/2 tsp · 1.5 g
  • garlic, lightly crushed3 cloves · 6 g
  • dried red chillies, as Guntur karam2 chillies or 1 tsp · 2 g
  • curry leaves10 leaves · 1.5 g
  • onion, finely chopped2 tbsp · 30 g
  • green chilli, slit2 chillies · 8 g
  • turmeric1/4 tsp · 1 g
  • saltabout 1/2 tsp · 2.5 g
Method
  1. Pick the fenugreek leaves from the stems, wash and drain well, then chop fine. Beat the eggs with half the salt and a pinch of turmeric.
  2. Heat the ghee in a kadai over medium heat. Add cumin; when it sizzles and darkens (about 10 seconds), add the garlic, dried chillies and curry leaves and fry until the garlic turns pale gold and the leaves stop spluttering, about 40 seconds.
  3. Add the onion and green chilli and fry 2 minutes until translucent with brown edges.
  4. Add the fenugreek leaves, remaining turmeric and salt. Toss on medium-high for 2 to 3 minutes until the leaves collapse and the pan looks nearly dry. This is what removes the raw bitterness.
  5. Reduce to medium, pour in the eggs and let them sit 20 seconds, then fold gently every 15 seconds for 2 to 3 minutes. Cook until no liquid egg remains and the curds are firm (160 F / 71 C); stop there so they stay tender.
  6. Rest 1 minute and serve hot, with a squeeze of lemon if you like.
Make it taste like home
  • Salting the chopped leaves for 5 minutes and squeezing them softens bitterness further, but costs some flavour; the hard wilt in step 4 is usually enough.
  • Keto: a measured 30 g of onion keeps this breakfast low; no tomato here, so the dish stays dry and fast.
  • Make ahead: the fenugreek-onion base (steps 2 to 4) can be cooked the night before and refrigerated in a covered container up to 2 days. Reheat to steaming and add the eggs fresh. Cooked egg dishes keep refrigerated for 3 to 4 days and must be reheated until steaming hot (165 F / 74 C).
Swaps
  • Mild: use one green chilli and 1/2 tsp chilli powder.
  • Dairy-free: use sesame or groundnut oil instead of ghee.
