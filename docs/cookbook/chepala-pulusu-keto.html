Keto Chepala Pulusu (Tomato and Tiny-Tamarind Fish Stew)చేపల పులుసు (కీటో)

The tangy, spicy Andhra fish stew with the sourness built from ripe tomato and just 6 g of tamarind pulp for the whole pot, thickened with a spoon of poppy-seed paste. It tastes even better the next day.

  • 8.2 g net carbs
  • 33 g protein
  • 21 g fat
  • 367 kcal
  • 29 min
  • Serves 2
Fat 54%Protein 37%Net carbs 9%per serving
Ingredients
  • catfish fillet or steaks (US farmed channel catfish), 4 to 5 cm chunksabout 14 oz · 400 g
  • lemon juice, for the marinade1 tsp · 5 g
  • Guntur red chilli powder2 tsp, plus 1/4 tsp for the fish · 4 g
  • turmeric1/2 tsp · 1 g
  • salt3/4 tsp · 4 g
  • tamarind pulp, seedless, weighed on a scale6 g, about a marble-sized piece · 6 g
  • poppy seeds (gasagasalu), ground to a paste with 40 ml water2 tsp · 5 g
  • sesame (gingelly) oil1 tbsp · 14 g
  • mustard seeds1/4 tsp · 1 g
  • cumin seeds1/4 tsp · 1 g
  • fenugreek seeds1/4 tsp · 1 g
  • dried red chilli1 chilli · 1 g
  • curry leaves2 sprigs · 3 g
  • green chillies, slit2 chillies · 8 g
  • onion, finely chopped2 tbsp · 30 g
  • garlic, minced3 cloves · 8 g
  • ginger, minced1 1/2 cm piece · 5 g
  • ripe tomato, finely chopped1 small tomato · 80 g
  • coriander (dhaniya) powder1 1/2 tsp · 3 g
  • garam masala3/4 tsp · 1.5 g
  • coriander leaves with tender stalksa small handful · 6 g
Method
  1. Prep (9 min): put the 6 g of tamarind in a small bowl with 120 ml hot water, soak 5 minutes, then squeeze and strain to get a sour liquid; discard the fibres. Toss the fish with 1 tsp lemon juice, 1/4 tsp chilli powder, a pinch of turmeric and a pinch of salt and leave it while you work. Grind the poppy seeds to a fine powder, then to a paste with 40 ml water.
  2. Heat the sesame oil in a wide shallow pan over medium heat. Add the mustard seeds; when they crackle, add the cumin, fenugreek and dried chilli for 10 seconds, then the curry leaves and green chillies until the leaves stop spluttering.
  3. Add the onion, garlic and ginger and fry 3 minutes until golden at the edges. Add the tomato and a pinch of salt and cook 3 to 4 minutes, pressing it with the spoon, until it collapses and the oil begins to float on top.
  4. Stir in the remaining chilli powder, the turmeric and the coriander powder for 30 seconds with a splash of water so the spices do not scorch.
  5. Pour in the tamarind liquid plus 100 ml more water and the remaining salt. Bring to a boil and cook 3 minutes so the raw tamarind smell cooks off and the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Slide in the fish pieces in a single layer. Do not stir from now on. Cover and simmer gently 6 to 7 minutes, swirling the pan once. The fish is cooked when the pieces flake at the edge with a fork.
  7. Add the poppy-seed paste, the garam masala and the coriander with its stalks. Tilt and swirl the pan, simmer 1 minute, then take it off the heat. Remove the stalks and let it rest at least 10 minutes before serving with cauliflower rice.
Make it taste like home
  • Counted tamarind: 6 g of tamarind pulp is about 3.4 g net carbs across the whole pot, so under 2 g per serving. If you want more sour, add a squeeze of lemon at the table (about 0.3 g net carbs per teaspoon) instead of more tamarind.
  • Fish choices at US stores: US-farmed catfish holds its shape in a stew; pomfret steaks, kingfish (surmai/vanjaram) and rohu or katla from the frozen section of Indian grocers are the closest to the Andhra original; salmon works but is richer. Delicate tilapia fillets break up, so add them for the last 5 to 7 minutes, covered, over low heat, and do not stir.
  • Keto tip: ground poppy seeds thicken the stew the way coconut or cashew paste does in other versions, without the carbs of a flour or dal thickener.
  • Make ahead: pulusu improves overnight; store covered in the fridge for 2 days and reheat gently without stirring hard so the fish does not break.
Swaps
  • For a milder pot, use 2 tsp of the chilli powder and one green chilli.
  • Replace the poppy seeds with 2 tbsp thin coconut milk (check the label for no added sugar) if you cannot find them.
