Kodi Gasagasala Kura (Pressure-Cooker Poppy-Coconut Chicken)

A rich, nutty Andhra chicken curry thickened with poppy seeds and coconut, with dried red chilli heat. The pressure cooker brings it together in about half an hour.

8.9g net carbs  ·  43g protein  ·  26g fat  ·  464 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 53% ● Protein 39% ● Net Carbs 8%
Ingredients
  • boneless chicken thigh, 3 cm pieces 400g
  • ghee 4 tsp · 20g
  • onion, sliced 50g
  • tomato, chopped 50g
  • poppy seeds, soaked 10 min in hot water 1 tbsp · 12g
  • grated coconut 25g
  • fennel seeds 2g
  • dried red chilli 3 chillies · 4g
  • garlic 10g
  • ginger 10g
  • red chilli powder 5g
  • turmeric 2g
  • garam masala 2g
  • coriander seeds 4g
  • green chilli, slit 10g
  • curry leaves 4g
  • coriander leaves 5g
  • salt 4g
Method
  1. Blend soaked poppy seeds, coconut, fennel, coriander seeds, 2 dried chillies and 5 tbsp water into a fine paste.
  2. Heat ghee in the open cooker, add remaining dried chilli, curry leaves and green chilli until the leaves stop spluttering, then onion; fry 4 to 5 minutes until deep golden.
  3. Add ginger and garlic (chopped) for 1 minute, then tomato, chilli powder, turmeric and salt; cook until the oil separates, about 3 minutes.
  4. Add chicken and stir 2 minutes, then the paste and 1/2 cup water. Close and pressure cook 2 whistles (about 8 minutes) on medium heat.
  5. Release pressure naturally for 5 minutes. Simmer uncovered 3 to 4 minutes to thicken; check chicken reads 74 C / 165 F in the thickest piece.
  6. Stir in garam masala and coriander and rest 5 minutes.
Make it taste like home
  • Soaking poppy seeds gives a smooth, creamy gravy.
  • Keto tip: thickening comes from poppy and coconut, with no flour or cashew.
  • Storage: cool within 2 hours; refrigerate up to 3 days; reheat until steaming hot (74 C / 165 F). It tastes better the next day.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Use 2 dried chillies and skip the green chilli for heat 3.
