Tomato Guddu Vepudu (Guntur Tomato Egg Fry)టమాటో గుడ్డు వేపుడు

Soft egg curds catch a reduced tomato-onion masala, with a sharp mustard-and-curry-leaf talimpu and a final hit of Guntur chilli. Keep it just saucy enough to cling to each bite.

5.0g net carbs  ·  14g protein  ·  14g fat  ·  211 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 63% ● Protein 27% ● Net Carbs 10%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Tomato Egg Curry. #egg #eggcurry #eggrecipe #eggrecipes #curry #telugucooking — Anu’s Kitchen
Ingredients
  • eggs, beaten 4 large · 200g
  • ripe tomato, finely chopped 1 small · 100g
  • onion, finely chopped 2 tbsp · 20g
  • ghee 1 1/2 tsp · 8g
  • mustard seeds 1/2 tsp · 1g
  • cumin seeds 1/2 tsp · 1.5g
  • fresh curry leaves 1 sprig · 2g
  • green chilli, finely sliced 2 small · 8g
  • garlic, minced 2 cloves · 5g
  • Guntur red chilli powder about 1 tsp · 2g
  • turmeric powder 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • coriander powder 1 tsp · 2g
  • fresh coriander leaves, chopped 1 tbsp · 4g
  • salt to taste · 2g
Method
  1. Beat the eggs with half the salt. Chop the tomato and onion finely so the masala reduces quickly.
  2. Heat ghee in a kadai over medium heat. Add mustard and cumin; when they crackle, add curry leaves and green chilli. Fry about 20 seconds, until the leaves stop spluttering.
  3. Add onion and fry 2 minutes until translucent with a few golden edges. Stir in garlic for 30 seconds, then add tomato, turmeric and remaining salt. Cook 3 to 4 minutes, pressing the tomato until pulpy and most of its liquid has evaporated.
  4. Turn the heat to low. Stir in the chilli and coriander powders for 10 seconds, then pour in the beaten eggs. Let them sit for 15 seconds; fold slowly until large, tender curds form and no wet egg remains, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat while the curds are still soft. Scatter coriander leaves over the top and rest 1 minute before serving.
Make it taste like home
  • Let the tomato masala reduce before the eggs go in; a watery base makes small, rubbery curds.
  • For a softer finish, turn off the heat while the eggs still look slightly glossy; the warm kadai will finish them.
  • Use one green chilli and half the chilli powder for a gentler plate.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Replace ghee with sesame oil for a dairy-free version.
  • Use chopped tomato rather than puree for a more textured fry.

Gasagasala Kobbari Goat Keema Kura (Poppy-Coconut Goat Mince Curry)గసగసాల కొబ్బరి మేక కీమా కూర

Lean goat keema cooks quickly into a peppery, clingy curry, rounded with a little coconut milk and a lightly toasted poppy-seed paste. Curry leaves and Guntur karam keep the finish unmistakably Andhra.

5.0g net carbs  ·  33g protein  ·  14g fat  ·  289 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 46% ● Protein 47% ● Net Carbs 7%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
అసలైన హైదరాబాద్ స్పెషల్ ధం ఖీమా | Hyderabad Special Dum Keema | Mutton Keema Recipe — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • lean goat mince about 10 1/2 oz · 300g
  • onion, finely chopped about 3 tbsp · 25g
  • ripe tomato, finely chopped 1/2 small · 45g
  • fresh ginger, minced 1 cm piece · 4g
  • garlic, minced 2 cloves · 6g
  • ghee 2 tsp · 10g
  • fresh curry leaves 1 sprig · 2g
  • green chilli, slit 2 small · 8g
  • Guntur red chilli powder 1 1/2 tsp · 3g
  • coriander powder 1 1/2 tsp · 3g
  • cumin seeds 1/2 tsp · 1.5g
  • white poppy seeds 2 tsp · 6g
  • unsweetened canned coconut milk 2 tbsp plus 1 tsp · 35g
  • turmeric powder 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • fresh coriander leaves, chopped 1 tbsp · 4g
  • salt to taste · 3g
Method
  1. Toast the poppy seeds in a dry pan over low heat for 60 to 90 seconds, shaking often, just until fragrant; do not let them brown. Cool, then grind with 2 tablespoons water to a smooth paste.
  2. Heat ghee in a kadai over medium. Add cumin and curry leaves; when they crackle and the leaves turn glossy, add onion and green chilli. Fry 3 minutes until the onion has golden edges.
  3. Add ginger and garlic; stir 30 seconds until aromatic. Add the goat mince and break it into small pieces. Fry 4 minutes, stirring, until it loses its raw colour and the pan gives off a meaty aroma.
  4. Add chilli powder, coriander powder, turmeric and salt. Stir for 15 seconds, then add tomato and 2 tablespoons water. Cover and cook on low for 8 minutes, stirring once; the mince should release enough liquid to stay moist.
  5. Stir in the poppy paste and coconut milk. Simmer uncovered 3 to 4 minutes, stirring often, until the pale gravy darkens, thickens and clings to the keema. Finish with coriander leaves and rest 2 minutes.
Make it taste like home
  • Keep the poppy seeds pale while toasting: even a little scorching makes their finish bitter.
  • Check that the canned coconut milk is unsweetened and stir it smooth before measuring.
  • The keema reheats well in a covered pan with a spoonful of water; bring it back to a gentle simmer.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Use grated fresh coconut in place of the coconut milk, thinning the poppy paste with water.
  • For a deeper pepper note, finish with a pinch of freshly ground black pepper.

Gongura Chepa Vepudu with Curry-Leaf Cauliflower Riceగోంగూర చేపల వేపుడు

Flaky white fish gets a quick Guntur-spice sear, then meets a garlicky gongura fry whose tart leaves balance the rich tempering. Curry-leaf cauliflower rice catches every last bit of the pan masala.

9.9g net carbs  ·  28g protein  ·  20g fat  ·  337 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 54% ● Protein 34% ● Net Carbs 12%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
GonguraChepalaPulusu|Sorrel Fish Masala|గోంగూరా ఈ మసాలాలు తో పులుసు పెడితే మళ్ళీ ఇదే కావాలంటారు — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • catfish fillets, patted dry about 10 1/2 oz · 300g
  • gongura leaves, picked and chopped about 3 packed cups · 120g
  • cauliflower florets, riced and squeezed dry about 3 cups riced · 300g
  • groundnut oil 2 tsp · 10g
  • ghee 1 1/2 tsp · 8g
  • green chilli, slit 2 small · 8g
  • garlic, minced 3 cloves · 8g
  • fresh ginger, minced 1 cm piece · 4g
  • fresh curry leaves 1 sprig · 2g
  • mustard seeds 1/2 tsp · 1g
  • cumin seeds 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • Guntur red chilli powder 1 tsp · 2g
  • turmeric powder 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • lemon juice 1 1/2 tsp · 8g
  • fresh coriander leaves, chopped 1 tbsp · 4g
  • salt to taste · 3g
Method
  1. Grate the cauliflower to rice-sized pieces, then squeeze it dry in a clean towel. Pat the fish dry and rub with turmeric, chilli powder, lemon juice and half the salt; set aside while the pan heats.
  2. In a wide skillet, heat ghee over medium-high. Add cumin and curry leaves; once fragrant, tip in the cauliflower and a pinch of salt. Toss over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes until the grains are hot, separate and just tender. Transfer to a covered bowl.
  3. Wipe the skillet if needed and heat the groundnut oil over medium-high. Add the fish pieces in one layer and sear 3 to 4 minutes per side, turning once, until opaque and flaky. Transfer to a plate.
  4. Lower the heat to medium. Add mustard seeds; when they pop, add green chilli, ginger and garlic. Stir 30 seconds, then add the chopped gongura and remaining salt. Cover for 2 minutes to wilt, then uncover and cook 3 to 4 minutes until the leaves collapse and the pan is nearly dry.
  5. Fold the fish back into the gongura for 30 seconds, keeping the pieces intact. Scatter coriander over the fish and serve beside the curry-leaf cauliflower rice.
Make it taste like home
  • Dry the cauliflower well before frying; moisture steams it and keeps the grains from separating.
  • Gongura's sourness varies by bunch. Taste after wilting and add lemon only if the leaves need a brighter finish.
  • Use a broad turner and flip the catfish only once so the pieces stay whole.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Use tilapia or another firm white fish in place of catfish.
  • For a lower-heat plate, omit the green chilli and use 1 g chilli powder.
