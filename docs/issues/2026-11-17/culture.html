Veteran Telugu actor, producer and director Giri Babu dies at 80 in Hyderabad  —  Veteran Telugu cinema actor, producer, and director Giri Babu died at 80 on November 14 at a private hospital in Kondapur, Hyderabad, after a prolonged age-related illness. Born on June 8, 1946, in Prakasam district and debuting in Jagame Maya, he built a multi-decade career across lead, antagonist, and character roles while producing films such as Simha Garjana, Merupudadi, and Indrajith and directing titles including Ranarangam and Nee Sukhame Ne Korukunna.
