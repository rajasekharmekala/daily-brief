Tollywood and Telugu cinema

Sacnilk: yesterday · 3m
Nani's 'The Paradise' arrives on Netflix on October 22 in five languages  —  Netflix India confirmed on October 18 that Srikanth Odela's period action drama, released in theatres on September 24, streams from October 22 in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam; the last two are new to the film. It is set in a 1980s Secunderabad slum and has an Anirudh score. Trade tracking puts its worldwide gross at about Rs 173 crore, with a very front-loaded run and divided reactions to the storytelling and violence.
Takeaway: A Telugu film with an Anirudh score landing on Netflix this week, which fits your Telugu cinema and OTT watchlist.
The Week: yesterday · 3m
Jailer 2 reaches Rs 477.75 crore worldwide in five days and sets a Tamil first-Monday record  —  Rajinikanth and Nelson's sequel, released October 15 with an Anirudh soundtrack, had grossed Rs 477.75 crore worldwide by 10 pm on day 5 according to Sacnilk, after Rs 424.25 crore in the first four days. Its first Monday, over Rs 45 crore domestic gross, is the biggest for a Tamil film, ahead of Leo at Rs 39.5 crore. The Telugu version netted about Rs 6.46 crore that day, and Dussehra holidays should help the next two days.
Takeaway: Anirudh scored it and it is running in Telugu as well as Tamil, so it is the big Indian-cinema story of the week for your Anirudh follow list.
Telangana Today: today · 2m
Venkatesh and Trivikram's 'Aadarsha Kutumbam' gets a new release date: October 30  —  The makers of 'Aadarsha Kutumbam House No: 47 - AK 47' announced on October 20 that the Telugu family entertainer opens worldwide on October 30. It was first dated October 2, then October 9, so this is the second delay. It is Trivikram's first film directing Venkatesh in the lead, with Srinidhi Shetty and Nara Rohith in the cast.
Takeaway: The next big Telugu theatrical release, ten days out, for your Telugu cinema list.
DT Next: 2 days ago · 2m
Karthik Subbaraj's 'Dorothy' heads to Netflix on October 23, with a Telugu version  —  Karthik Subbaraj's Tamil drama, which starred Keerthy Suresh, Sananth and Rishikanth, premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 11 and opened in theatres on September 25. It reaches Netflix on October 23 in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Hindi. Ilaiyaraaja composed the music, with Karthik Subbaraj producing alongside Sikhya Entertainment and Jio Studios.
Takeaway: Broad interest in Indian cinema and music: a Tamil film arriving in Telugu on Netflix with an Ilaiyaraaja score.
India Today: 6 days ago · 4m
Is Anirudh losing his edge? A critic weighs the Jailer 2 album  —  An opinion piece on the muted reaction to the Jailer 2 jukebox, arguing it has no song with the reach of 'Hukum' from the first film. It holds up 'Coolie' and 'DC' as recent albums where songs and score worked together, and notes the heavy synth basslines and percussion that many listeners now find similar across his albums. It also suggests tight production schedules may leave little time for final re-recording.
Takeaway: You follow Anirudh, and this is a useful counterpoint to the film's box-office headlines.

Hollywood, series and anime

IGN: yesterday · 4m
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 reaches Netflix, and IGN calls it spectacular if less monumental than season one  —  IGN reviews the standalone Night City sequel, out on Netflix on October 20. It follows several storylines, chiefly a downgraded ex-edgerunner and a 12-year-old amateur filmmaker, and swaps season one's class-warfare theme for a plot about corporations manipulating lives. The reviewer finds the characters less relatable and the plot sprawling at first, but praises the Studio Trigger animation and fight scenes. No need to have seen season one or the game.
Takeaway: Fits your anime watching alongside Attack on Titan, Demon Slayer and Solo Leveling, and it is a sci-fi action series that works without prior viewing.
Tom's Guide (via Yahoo Entertainment): 5 days ago · 5m
The Diplomat season 4 is streaming on Netflix, and Tom's Guide rates it the show's best yet  —  All eight episodes of the Netflix political thriller are available at once. Tom's Guide gives season 4 a rating of 4.5 out of 5, praising the intricate plotting, the Wyler marriage at the centre, and larger roles for Allison Janney and Bradley Whitford as the president and first gentleman. The story picks up the fallout from a secret operation involving Russia's Poseidon weapon. The reviewer notes a few minor missteps.
Takeaway: Matches the political and government dramas on your watch history, such as Designated Survivor, Madam Secretary and House of Cards.
Los Angeles Times: Oct 1 · 5m
HBO's War sets two law firms against each other in a celebrity divorce  —  The LA Times reviews this HBO legal drama from George Kay, which premiered Thursday October 1 as the first instalment of an anthology. Dominic West plays a tech mogul and Sienna Miller a film star; their lawyers face off over eight episodes, moving from mediation to arbitration to court. Critic Robert Lloyd calls it satisfying and compares it to The Good Wife or The Good Fight, treating the divorce itself as a device to set characters in motion.
Takeaway: Fits your appetite for legal and courtroom drama, from Suits to Law & Order, and its firm-versus-firm format is built around the case rather than a single detective.
Apple TV Press: Sep 29 · 3m
Apple TV sets Nocturne, a serial-killer crime drama with Liev Schreiber and Stephen Graham, for October 30  —  Apple TV released the trailer for its ten-episode crime drama based on Lars Kepler's novel The Sandman. Liev Schreiber plays an ex-soldier turned homicide detective who moves to a small town in Western Pennsylvania, where the serial killer Jurek Walter (Stephen Graham) targets his family; Zazie Beetz plays the FBI agent he sends after the killer. The first two episodes arrive on Friday October 30, then one a week through December 25.
Takeaway: A detective-versus-serial-killer series in the vein of the crime thrillers you look up, such as Mindhunter and True Detective.
Gold Derby: today · 4m
Lee Chang-dong's Possible Love opens in select US theaters October 23, then streams on Netflix November 6  —  The Burning director's first film in eight years won the Silver Lion Grand Jury Prize at Venice and is South Korea's submission for Best International Feature at the 2027 Oscars. Gold Derby reports a 100% Certified Fresh score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 93 on Metacritic. Two married couples from opposite ends of Korean society collide when a wealthy documentary filmmaker films a laid-off worker's wife. Netflix has just released a new trailer.
Takeaway: Fits your interest in Korean drama and critically praised films; if you skip the cinema, it arrives on Netflix on November 6.
