World

Al Jazeera: 3 days ago · 5m
US and Iran both claim control of the Strait of Hormuz as ship attacks climb  —  October has become the busiest month for attacks on shipping around Hormuz since the war began on 28 February. UKMTO logged at least 12 attacks in the week to 16 October and says many go unreported. Kpler put flows at 11.6 million barrels a day, about 68% of the pre-war 17.1 million. CENTCOM says the US controls the strait; Iran says ships bypassing its authority risk mines. Mediators such as Pakistan and Qatar still relay messages, but no breakthrough is expected soon.
Takeaway: Middle East, Iran and Hormuz are core to your geopolitics list, and this gives the clearest picture of who actually controls the shipping lane and how much oil is moving.
CNBC: yesterday · 5m
Putin reportedly floats a Trump-Xi-Putin summit on Ukraine, without Zelenskyy  —  The Wall Street Journal reports that Putin proposed, in an Oct. 9 call with Trump, a three-way meeting with Trump and Xi to negotiate an end to the Ukraine war; it could be held around the November APEC summit in Shenzhen. Rubio confirmed talks of a meeting are under way. Meanwhile US-Ukraine friction is growing: Zelenskyy says intelligence sharing was briefly interrupted, which Rubio denies, and Trump keeps pressing Kyiv to stop striking Russian energy sites.
Takeaway: Russia-Ukraine diplomacy and a possible US-China-Russia meeting touch two named threads in your geopolitics interests: the Ukraine war and China.
TIME: 3 days ago · 5m
What Trump's waiver of the new Russia sanctions means for Ukraine and energy markets  —  On Friday 16 October Trump signed an order waiving the new sanctions and tariffs in the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act ahead of their 18 October start; the law allowed tariffs of up to 100% on major buyers of Russian oil and gas. It follows a diesel deal with Russia backed by a temporary US licence through April 7, 2027. Quoted experts say Moscow's main gain is strategic rather than financial, and lawmakers in both parties object.
Takeaway: Links Russia sanctions, energy supply and US politics, three of the topics you follow under geopolitics (Russia/Ukraine, energy, US policy).
CNBC: yesterday · 4m
Saudi Arabia postpones its flagship investor conference as Houthi attacks escalate  —  Riyadh pushed its Future Investment Initiative conference, due Oct. 26-29, to 2027 after weeks of Houthi missile and drone strikes. The Houthis hit King Khalid International Airport on Oct. 10, killing at least 12 and injuring more than 300, and on Monday claimed fresh attacks on that airport, Abha airport, Aramco sites at Yanbu and the Shaybah oilfield. Saudi forces say they downed several drones over the weekend. CNBC describes the US-Iran war spreading across the region.
Takeaway: A second front in the Iran-linked conflict that directly affects Gulf energy infrastructure, relevant to your Middle East and energy interests.
Fortune (Associated Press): 3 days ago · 4m
Democrats make the Iran war a central theme in competitive midterm races  —  An AP analysis of AdImpact data finds Iran-war ads in at least half of 50 competitive House and Senate races, airing on TV more than 43,000 times since Sept. 1. Of 65 unique ads, 61 stress cost-of-living themes such as fuel prices. Republicans mostly avoid the topic and focus on taxes and crime. Seven House Republicans joined Democrats in the latest vote to end the war, four of them in tight districts.
Takeaway: Shows how the Iran war is feeding into US politics ahead of the Nov. 3 midterms, which sits within your US politics and policy interest.
OilPrice.com: 3 days ago · 4m
Oil futures now price a longer war: Brent stays elevated into 2027  —  Early in the war most traders expected a quick end; now Brent futures imply tight supply into 2027. The January 2027 contract trades near $102.59, June 2027 near $92.71 and December 2027 near $84.75, against under $70 in December 2025. The piece argues that reported recoveries in Gulf flows have not cut prices for long, because of doubts over official figures and costly ship-to-ship transfers that raised freight costs, with diesel especially tight.
Takeaway: Energy supply and the Hormuz disruption are named in your geopolitics interests; this explains how long the energy-market side of the conflict is expected to last.

India

The New Indian Express: 5 days ago · 3m
India says first tranche of US trade pact is finalised and in legal drafting  —  Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said on 15 October that the first tranche of the India-US bilateral trade agreement is finalised and in legal drafting, but will be operationalised only once India gets a competitive edge over peer countries. The commerce secretary tied signing partly to the US settling its tariff framework, and US Ambassador Sergio Gor said the big items are done. Goyal also said the India-EU trade pact should be signed in December.
Takeaway: India-US relations and trade policy are one of the India topics you follow.
The Hindu BusinessLine: 5 days ago · 4m
Telangana draws up El Niño action plan as reservoirs and rainfall fall short  —  Chief Minister Revanth Reddy said on 15 October that Telangana has an action plan for the next eight to ten months of El Niño stress. The state has a 23 per cent rainfall deficit, Krishna basin storage is down 72 per cent and Godavari 40 per cent, and power demand hit 20,000 MW against about 15,000 MW expected. Farmers are urged to cut rabi paddy; drinking water is needed for 24,000 villages.
Takeaway: Covers Telangana, one of the two Telugu states you follow, and a story that may spread to Andhra Pradesh through power and water supply.
Mint: 5 days ago · 4m
RBI explains its 20% reserve rule on rupee hedging contracts  —  On 15 October the RBI published FAQs on its Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve, which follows directions of 10 and 12 October. Banks must hold a non-interest-bearing reserve equal to 20% of the rupee value of eligible currency derivatives. Contracts of $2 million or less, hedges where users sell foreign currency, contracts not involving the rupee and capital-account hedges are outside the rule. Daily reporting is due by 5 pm next working day.
Takeaway: RBI policy moves are a core part of the India economy coverage you follow.
The Hindu: 6 days ago · 3m
Andhra Pradesh sets local body poll dates: 100 civic bodies vote on 28 October  —  The Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission announced a phased local body schedule that runs to 2 December. Polls for 100 urban bodies, including 13 municipal corporations such as Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada, are on 28 October with results on 30 October. MPTC and ZPTC voting follows on 3 November, and gram panchayat polls on 26 and 29 November and 2 December. The model code now bars new welfare schemes.
Takeaway: Andhra Pradesh politics is part of the India coverage you follow, and the first round of voting is eight days away.
The Guardian: Sep 26 · 9m
Inside Google's $15bn AI data centre in an Andhra Pradesh village  —  A Guardian report from Tarluvada, near Visakhapatnam, on Google's $15bn AI data centre built with the Adani Group. The project was announced as 1GW, but state environmental clearances allow 2.51GW, which the article puts at upwards of 30% of the current annual energy use of Andhra Pradesh. Villagers say land was taken back and officials have not answered their questions; Google calls it a gigawatt-scale AI hub.
Takeaway: Joins your Andhra Pradesh coverage with large-scale AI infrastructure, a topic close to your agent and AI interests.
The Hindu: yesterday · 3m
Revanth Reddy orders Invest Telangana summit venue ready by mid-November  —  On 19 October the Telangana chief minister toured Bharat Future City and told officials to finish preparations for the Invest Telangana Global Summit, set for 7 to 9 December, by the end of November. Open-venue work should be done by 15 November, and the Young India Skills University is to finish at least 15 days early. The theme is "Your Investments, Our Assurance"; a dedicated oversight committee was requested.
Takeaway: Tracks Hyderabad and Telangana infrastructure and investment, which you follow.
