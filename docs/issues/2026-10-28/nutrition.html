Paneer Nuvvula Vepudu (Sesame-Karam Paneer Fry)పనీర్ నువ్వుల వేపుడు

Paneer seared until it blisters at the edges, then tossed in a freshly ground roasted-sesame, red-chilli and garlic karam that clings like a crust. It is the breakfast version of the spoon of nuvvula podi on an Andhra plate: nutty, hot and ready in 20 minutes.

5.9g net carbs  ·  22g protein  ·  36g fat  ·  437 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 75% ● Protein 20% ● Net Carbs 5%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Quick Paneer Stir Fry 😋 #paneer #food #stirfry #trending #bollywood — Benz Fairy
Ingredients
  • paneer, cut into 2 cm cubes and patted dry about 1 small block · 200g
  • ghee 1 tbsp · 14g
  • sesame (nuvvula) oil 1 tsp · 5g
  • white sesame seeds, for the karam 1 tbsp · 12g
  • dried red chillies (Guntur or byadgi) 3 to 4 chillies · 3g
  • cumin seeds 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • coriander powder about 1 tsp · 2g
  • garlic, 3 small cloves 3 small cloves · 4g
  • curry leaves 1 sprig · 3g
  • mustard seeds 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • turmeric powder a pinch · 0.5g
  • lemon juice 1.5 tsp · 8g
  • fresh coriander leaves a small handful · 8g
  • salt 1/3 tsp · 2g
Method
  1. Pat the paneer cubes very dry with a towel and toss them with the turmeric and half the salt. Dry surfaces brown; wet ones steam.
  2. Make the karam: in a dry skillet over medium heat toast the sesame seeds, stirring constantly, until they turn light golden and smell nutty, about 90 seconds. Tip them out. In the same pan toast the dried chillies and cumin for 30 seconds until the chillies darken a shade and the cumin smells sharp. Cool 2 minutes.
  3. Grind the chillies, cumin, coriander powder and the remaining salt to a fine powder, then add the sesame and garlic and pulse only 3 to 5 times so the mix stays coarse and a few seeds survive; going further turns it into a paste.
  4. Heat the ghee in the skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the paneer in a single layer and leave it alone for about 90 seconds until the underside is golden brown, then turn the cubes and brown the other sides, 3 to 4 minutes in all. Lift them onto a plate.
  5. Drop the heat to medium. Add the sesame oil and mustard seeds; when they stop popping, about 15 seconds, add the curry leaves and fry until they stop spluttering and turn glassy.
  6. Return the paneer, sprinkle over the karam and toss for 45 to 60 seconds until every cube is coated and the powder smells toasted, not burnt. Keep the heat moderate so the chilli does not scorch.
  7. Turn off the heat, squeeze in the lemon juice, add the coriander leaves and let it sit 2 minutes before serving.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Guntur or byadgi dried red chillies: Any dried red chillies (arbol, or chile de arbol from the Mexican aisle) plus 1/2 tsp cayenne for the heat
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara), New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale), or the spice aisle at Safeway or Whole Foods
  • Fresh curry leaves: Frozen curry leaves; dried leaves are a weak last resort
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Frozen aisle at Patel Brothers or New India Bazaar
  • Sesame (nuvvula) oil: Toasted sesame oil, 1/2 tsp, or more ghee
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Any Asian aisle, 99 Ranch or Safeway
  • Block paneer: Any full-fat paneer or halloumi, which is saltier so cut the added salt
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers, Trader Joe's (halloumi), Costco
Make it taste like home
  • Heat 4 as written. For heat 2 use 1 dried chilli plus 1/4 tsp paprika; for heat 5 add 1/2 tsp cayenne to the karam.
  • Keto: the karam is the flavour, and 12 g of sesame costs under 2 g net carbs for the pan. Check the paneer label because carbs differ by brand; the pipeline uses a package label of 3.3 g per 100 g.
  • Make ahead: the karam keeps 5 days in a jar in the fridge. Fry the paneer fresh; it turns rubbery if reheated.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Firm tofu, pressed, for a dairy-free version, using sesame oil in place of ghee.
  • Quarter the dried chillies and add one sliced green chilli for a fresher, sharper heat.

Kanava Masala Iguru (Andhra Squid Dry Masala)కనవ మసాలా ఇగురు

Tender squid rings cooked fast in a thick coconut-oil masala of ginger, garlic, curry leaf and pepper until the sauce clings and the oil glistens. It is a dry iguru, the kind you eat with a spoon, and it takes about 25 minutes.

9.2g net carbs  ·  22g protein  ·  12g fat  ·  238 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 46% ● Protein 38% ● Net Carbs 16%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Squid Masala| Calamari Masala| How to make squid masala | Sea Food | Kanava Recipes @HomeCookingShow — HomeCookingShow
Ingredients
  • cleaned squid, tubes cut into 1 cm rings, tentacles kept about 2 medium squid · 260g
  • coconut oil 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp · 18g
  • fennel seeds 1/2 tsp · 1.5g
  • cumin seeds 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • curry leaves 1 sprig · 3g
  • onion, finely chopped about 2 tbsp · 25g
  • tomato, finely chopped about 1/2 small tomato · 50g
  • garlic, finely chopped 2 cloves · 6g
  • ginger, finely chopped 1.5 cm piece · 6g
  • green chilli, slit 2 Indian green chillies · 8g
  • Guntur karam or cayenne 1 tsp · 2.5g
  • coriander powder 1 tsp · 3g
  • turmeric powder 1/4 tsp · 0.7g
  • garam masala 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • black pepper, freshly ground 1/2 tsp · 1.5g
  • lemon juice 2 tsp · 10g
  • fresh coriander leaves a small handful · 6g
  • salt 1/3 tsp · 2g
Method
  1. Rinse the squid, pat it very dry and toss with the turmeric, half the salt and the lemon juice. Leave it 5 minutes while you chop; squid sheds water if it goes into the pan wet.
  2. Heat the coconut oil in the kadai over medium-high heat. Add the fennel and cumin; when they sizzle and smell toasty, about 15 seconds, add the curry leaves and slit chillies and fry until the leaves stop spluttering.
  3. Add the onion and cook 2 minutes until translucent with golden edges, then the garlic and ginger for 1 minute until the raw smell is gone.
  4. Add the tomato and a pinch of salt and cook 2 to 3 minutes, mashing with the spatula, until it collapses into a paste and a little oil shows at the edges. Stir in the karam and coriander powder for 20 seconds; keep the heat moderate so the karam does not burn.
  5. Raise the heat to high, add the squid and stir for 1 minute until the rings turn opaque. Cover and cook on medium-low for 5 minutes, until the pan is juicy.
  6. Uncover, raise the heat and cook 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until the juices reduce to a thick masala that coats the squid. Stop as soon as the juices have reduced, about 9 to 10 minutes of squid cooking in total; past about 12 minutes it turns rubbery.
  7. Stir in the pepper and garam masala, take the pan off the heat and finish with the coriander leaves. Rest 2 minutes and serve hot.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh cleaned squid: Frozen cleaned squid tubes (thaw in cold water 10 minutes, pat dry) or calamari rings
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: 99 Ranch, Costco, Trader Joe's frozen aisle, Whole Foods seafood counter
  • Fresh curry leaves: Frozen curry leaves; add a pinch more black pepper if you use none
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara) or New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale), frozen section
  • Guntur karam: Cayenne mixed with a little paprika for colour
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Any grocery spice aisle, or Patel Brothers
Make it taste like home
  • Heat 4 as written. For heat 2 drop the green chillies and use 1/2 tsp cayenne with 1 tsp paprika.
  • Keto: squid carries about 3 g carbohydrate per 100 g, so most of this dish's carbs come from the squid itself; keep onion and tomato to the measured amounts, and use lemon instead of tamarind.
  • Make ahead: chop the aromatics in the morning. Eat the same day; squid tightens if reheated hard, so warm it gently with a splash of water.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Peeled prawns (150 g) in place of squid, cooked 3 to 4 minutes in the finished masala.
  • Skip the green chillies for a milder dish.

Guddu Kobbari Nuvvula Kura (Egg in Roasted Coconut-Sesame Gravy)గుడ్డు కొబ్బరి నువ్వుల కూర

Boiled eggs blistered in groundnut oil, then simmered in a roasted coconut, sesame and red-chilli gravy finished with a splash of coconut milk. Deep, nutty and rounded with no tamarind at all, and it comes together in about 28 minutes.

8.1g net carbs  ·  22g protein  ·  39g fat  ·  473 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 74% ● Protein 19% ● Net Carbs 7%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Egg Masala Curry Recipe !!! — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled 6 eggs · 300g
  • groundnut oil 1 tbsp · 14g
  • unsweetened desiccated coconut, for the paste 2 tbsp · 15g
  • white sesame seeds, for the paste 2 tsp · 6g
  • dried red chillies 3 chillies · 3g
  • coriander seeds 2/3 tsp · 2g
  • fennel seeds 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • onion, finely chopped about 3 tbsp · 30g
  • tomato, finely chopped about 1/2 medium · 60g
  • garlic cloves 2 cloves · 6g
  • ginger 1 cm piece · 5g
  • curry leaves 1 sprig · 2g
  • mustard seeds 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • turmeric powder a pinch · 0.5g
  • garam masala 1/3 tsp · 1g
  • canned unsweetened coconut milk 1/3 cup · 80g
  • fresh coriander leaves a small handful · 6g
  • salt 1/2 tsp · 2.5g
Method
  1. Make the paste: in a dry kadai over medium heat toast the coconut until light golden, 1 to 2 minutes, then the sesame 45 seconds, then the dried chillies, coriander and fennel 30 seconds until fragrant. Cool 3 minutes and grind with the garlic, ginger and 4 tablespoons of water to a coarse paste.
  2. Pat the eggs dry, slit them with a fork, 3 or 4 pricks each, and toss with the turmeric and a pinch of salt. Heat half the oil in the kadai over medium-high heat and fry the eggs 60 to 90 seconds until the whites blister and turn golden. Lift them out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and mustard seeds; when they stop popping, add the curry leaves and onion and cook 3 minutes until the onion has golden edges.
  4. Add the tomato and a pinch of salt and cook 2 to 3 minutes until it collapses and the oil starts to separate at the edges.
  5. Stir in the ground paste and fry on medium-low for 4 to 5 minutes, scraping the bottom, until it darkens a shade and the oil separates. Add 120 ml of water and the garam masala and simmer 4 minutes.
  6. Add the coconut milk and the remaining salt and heat to a gentle simmer; do not boil hard or the milk may split. Slide in the eggs and spoon the gravy over them for 2 minutes, then finish with the coriander leaves and rest for 3 minutes before serving.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh grated coconut: Unsweetened desiccated coconut as written, or frozen grated coconut (use 25 g)
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara) or New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale); desiccated coconut at Whole Foods, Sprouts or Costco
  • Canned coconut milk: Unsweetened full-fat coconut milk with no added sugar; check the label, or use 40 ml heavy cream if you do not have it
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, Costco
  • Fresh curry leaves: Frozen curry leaves
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers or New India Bazaar, frozen section
Make it taste like home
  • Heat 4 as written. For heat 2 use 1 dried chilli plus 1/2 tsp paprika in the paste.
  • Keto: the thickness comes from toasted coconut and sesame, not flour or tamarind; measure the onion and tomato, because they carry most of the gravy's carbs.
  • Make ahead: the paste keeps 3 days in the fridge under a film of oil. The finished curry holds a day, though the eggs firm up on reheating, so warm gently.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Replace the eggs with 250 g pan-seared paneer for a vegetarian dinner.
  • Add 20 g chopped spinach in step 5 for extra greens.

Keto science and tips

Frontiers in Nutrition: Sep 11 · 8m
Ketogenic meal plans for adults leave gaps in fibre, vitamin D, B vitamins and potassium, a modelling study finds  —  A team at the University of Applied Sciences Muenster built 36 optimised hypothetical daily ketogenic meal plans (3:1, 2:1 and 1:1 ratios, four adult age groups) and compared them with German and Austrian reference values. Fibre, vitamin D, several B vitamins, potassium, zinc and fluoride fell short in all age groups; the looser 1:1 ratio did best. It is a desk model, not a trial of real eaters, and uses German food data.
Takeaway: Fibre and potassium are exactly what a strict-keto Andhra menu has to plan for; leafy greens, gongura, seeds and nuts are the levers worth checking.
BMC Cardiovascular Disorders (via Europe PMC record): Apr 1 · 6m
Ketogenic diets and blood lipids: a meta-regression of 53 randomized trials  —  Researchers from Minzu University of China and a Beijing hospital pooled 53 randomized trials in adults on ketogenic diets (under 10% carbohydrate). Versus control diets, triglycerides fell by 22.31 mg/dL and HDL rose 3.52 mg/dL, but LDL-C rose 8.22 mg/dL and total cholesterol 8.06 mg/dL. Certainty was moderate for triglycerides and HDL, low for LDL and total cholesterol. Long-term heart outcomes are unknown, and trial results varied widely.
Takeaway: General background on how keto-style diets affect blood lipids in trials, useful context when choosing cooking fats such as ghee, sesame, groundnut or coconut oil for the menu.
BMC Nutrition (University of Applied Sciences Muenster; via Europe PMC record): Feb 1 · 6m
Adverse events and tolerability of ketogenic diets: constipation and other gut complaints lead a systematic review  —  A Muenster team reviewed 36 prospective studies (42 ketogenic interventions, 2019-2024). At least one adverse event appeared in 43% of participants; gastrointestinal events were most common (40%), and constipation alone was 24%. Stricter diets brought more and more severe events, and tolerability varied with age, underlying disease and duration. The authors caution that diet definitions and event reporting differ a lot between studies.
Takeaway: General evidence on how often keto diets cause side effects such as constipation and that stricter versions are harder to sustain, which is background for fibre-rich menu design.
ScienceDaily (American Society for Nutrition): Aug 25 · 5m
Genes may decide how far LDL cholesterol rises on a low-carb diet high in saturated fat  —  Stanford researchers presented at NUTRITION 2026 an analysis of 431 DIETFITS trial participants with genetic data. Among those on the low-carb arm, people with a polygenic score for higher LDL showed larger LDL rises when they ate more saturated fat; the pattern was not seen on the low-fat arm. It was a 6-month secondary analysis and the authors say confirmation in more diverse groups is needed.
Takeaway: It bears on how you choose between ghee, coconut, groundnut and sesame oils in the Andhra keto menu, since the study points at fat choice rather than carbs alone.
Nutrition Reviews (University of Urbino, University of Padova; via Europe PMC record): Jun 2 · 6m
Ketogenic and low-carb diets and the gut microbiota: a narrative review finds mixed results  —  A review of 10 human studies covering 346 healthy adults. Lower carbohydrate intake went with taxonomic shifts in gut bacteria, but changes at phylum level (Bacteroidetes, Actinobacteria) were inconsistent, sometimes opposite under very-low-carbohydrate ketogenic protocols, and depended on how strict the diet was, how long it ran and how studies were done. The authors call for standardised, long-term human studies.
Takeaway: General background: effects of strict keto on gut bacteria are not settled, which supports keeping vegetables, seeds and other fibre sources in the menu.
