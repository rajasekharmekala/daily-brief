SFGATE: today · 4m
Vallarta Supermarkets Opens First Bay Area Store in San Jose With In-House Seafood Kitchen  —  Vallarta Supermarkets opened its first Bay Area location on November 18 at The Plant shopping center (2179 Monterey Road, San Jose), transforming a 63,000-square-foot former Toys R Us into a full-scale market and food court open daily 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Standout stops include a Marisquería Costa de Oro serving fish or shrimp ceviche and lime-cured aguachile, a custom $9.99-per-pound guacamole bar, a carnicería stocked with carne arrachera, house-made masa tamales and tortillas, and La Cocina hot entrees under $13.
Takeaway: Fresh San Jose food opening with low-carb-friendly aguachile, ceviche, custom guacamole, and grilled arrachera options.
Eventbrite / Open Space Authority: today · 2m
Hike Your Pie Off: Guided 5-Mile Walk Into Restricted South Rancho Cañada del Oro  —  On Friday, November 27 from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., the Santa Clara Valley Open Space Authority leads a moderate 5-mile guided hike through South Rancho (formerly Blair Ranch) at Rancho Cañada del Oro Open Space Preserve in Morgan Hill—a section not yet open for general public access. Docents share pond turtle and red-legged frog habitat restoration work, with a 4.7-mile Mayfair-Ranch to Longwall Trail backup route if trails are wet. Meet at the overflow parking lot; pets are not permitted.
Takeaway: Rare docent-led access to a restricted South Bay preserve trail within an easy drive of San Jose.
DoTheBay: today · 2m
West Coast Craft Winter Brings 275+ Independent Makers to Fort Mason Nov. 21–22  —  West Coast Craft's Winter market returns to the Festival Pavilion at Fort Mason Center in San Francisco on Saturday and Sunday, November 21–22, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. both days with free admission for all ages. The juried showcase gathers more than 275 independent artists and designer-craftspeople alongside local food vendors inside an indoor waterfront pavilion, making it an easy rain-proof weekend outing if Bay Area showers linger.
Takeaway: Vetted free indoor weekend market in San Francisco featuring 275+ independent craftspeople and local food.
The Silicon Valley Voice: today · 3m
Triton Holiday Art Fair and San Jose Stage’s 'CLUE' Headline South Bay Weekend  —  Local arts and community picks around Santa Clara and San Jose for November 20–22 include the Triton Holiday Art Fair on Saturday, November 21 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the Triton Museum of Art (1505 Warburton Ave., Santa Clara), and San Jose Stage's production of 'CLUE' running November 18 through December 20 at 490 S. 1st St. in downtown San Jose. Saturday morning shoppers can also visit the Sunnyvale Farmers' Market on Murphy Avenue or Santa Clara's market on Jackson Street (9 a.m.–1 p.m.).
Takeaway: Convenient close-to-home arts, theater, and Saturday farmers' market picks in Santa Clara and downtown San Jose.
7x7 Bay Area: Apr 28 · 4m
Peninsula and South Bay Hike-and-Dining Loops: Stanford Dish and Almaden Quicksilver  —  This evergreen Bay Area trail-and-table guide highlights two classic South Bay and Peninsula pairings within easy reach of San Jose: the iconic 3.7-mile paved Stanford Dish Loop Trail in Palo Alto—ideal for an early morning climb—paired with Eastern Mediterranean mezze at José Andrés' Zaytinya in Stanford Shopping Center, and the moderately challenging 3.6-mile English Camp Loop in Almaden Quicksilver County Park (21785 Almaden Rd., San Jose) paired with nearby La Foret in historic New Almaden.
Takeaway: Pairs fresh Peninsula and South Bay trails with standout Mediterranean and French dining spots for weekend planning.
