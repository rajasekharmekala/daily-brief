MIT Mathematics (Andrew Sutherland): today · 4m
Compressed 76,000-line Lean 4 Riemann Hypothesis proof passes MIT verification checks  —  Following the November 23 report that an Anthropic mathematician and Claude produced a 17-million-line Lean 4 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, a dramatically compressed version (`github.com/lsernau/grh-lean`, commit `72fead10`) was published on November 25. It ships in two Lean v4.35.0 editions—an 803-file, ~300,000-line "readable" build and a 794-file, ~76,000-line "compact" build—proving the same five Mathlib theorems. MIT's Andrew Sutherland verified both compile cleanly using only the three standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`) and pass independent checkers `nanoda`, `con-leche`, and `con-ron`, while paranoid comparator runs remain in progress.

Huawei: today · 3m
Huawei welcomes RICO acquittal, challenges remaining U.S. fraud and trade-secret convictions  —  Following yesterday's report on a Brooklyn federal jury convicting Huawei of trade-secrets conspiracy and bank fraud while acquitting it of racketeering, Huawei issued an official response from Shenzhen on November 25. The company welcomed the RICO acquittal as vindication of its eight-year defense, while asserting that the remaining convictions lack legal and factual merit. Huawei argued that the five cited trade-secret disputes were resolved civil matters over two decades with no court finding willful misappropriation, and pledged to review the verdicts thoroughly and pursue next legal steps while maintaining operations across 170+ countries.
