Badminton and tennis

BWF: today · 4m
BWF approves 2027–2028 format overhaul for World Championships and team cups  —  The Badminton World Federation approved major structural changes across its flagship championships starting in 2027, alongside its 3x15 scoring transition. From 2027, the BWF World Championships will move from straight knockout to an Olympic-style group stage followed by knockout rounds, guaranteeing every entrant at least two matches. Both the Sudirman Cup and the 2028 Thomas & Uber Cup Finals will expand to a 24-team league-and-knockout format where every match won earns standings points and tie wins add two bonus points, with the top 12 advancing. Notably, the Thomas & Uber Cup shifts to a best-of-three match format in 2028.
Takeaway: Directly reshapes how BWF major championships and team ties will be played across the badminton tour you follow closely.
Rediff Sports: today · 3m
HS Prannoy exits Syed Modi International in first round as Treesa-Gayatri advance  —  Wednesday's opening main-draw session at the Syed Modi India International Super 300 in Lucknow brought mixed results for Indian shuttlers. Former World Championships bronze medallist HS Prannoy suffered a first-round exit in men's singles, falling 19-21, 17-21 to World No. 80 Zhe Ying Wu of Chinese Taipei. In women's doubles, defending champions Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand opened their bid for a third consecutive title with a 21-8, 21-7 win over qualifiers Rashmi Ganesh and Amrutha Pramuthesh. In women's singles, third seed Unnati Hooda, fifth seed Ashmita Chaliha, Tanvi Sharma, Tanya Hemanth, Isharani Baruah, Tasnim Mir, and Anmol Kharb all moved into the second round.
Takeaway: Fresh Round 1 results for Indian shuttlers at the season's final regular BWF World Tour stop in Lucknow.
The Nation Thailand: yesterday · 3m
Kunlavut Vitidsarn reclaims men's singles World No. 1 ahead of Hangzhou Finals  —  Thailand's Kunlavut Vitidsarn returned to No. 1 in the BWF men's singles world rankings released Tuesday, moving up from second place with 91,585 points. He overtook France's Christo Popov, who slipped to second on 89,932 points. Vitidsarn gained the needed points by reaching the Hong Kong Open Super 500 final on November 22, even though a back injury forced him to concede the championship match to Denmark's Anders Antonsen by walkover. It marks the 25-year-old's fourth spell atop the rankings, though his fitness remains a watchpoint heading into December's BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou.
Takeaway: Key BWF men's singles rankings shift right before the World Tour Finals field gathers in Hangzhou.
Sportsnet: today · 2m
ITIA charges France's Arthur Rinderknech with refusing out-of-competition doping test  —  The International Tennis Integrity Agency confirmed Wednesday that 31-year-old French ATP player Arthur Rinderknech, currently ranked No. 57, has been charged with refusing to provide a doping test sample. According to French daily L'Equipe, the charge stems from an unannounced out-of-competition visit by doping control officers in June and carries a potential four-year ban—the same sanction handed to former Wimbledon champion Marketa Vondrousova in June. Rinderknech denied ever taking a banned substance on Instagram and remains eligible to compete while awaiting a tribunal hearing scheduled for early 2027.
Takeaway: Notable ATP integrity development during the tennis off-season that could affect the 2027 tour field.

F1, cricket and football

IPL Official: today · 3m
BCCI releases 1,220-player register for IPL 2027 auction with 66 slots open  —  The IPL released its preliminary Auction Register on Wednesday for the TATA IPL 2027 Player Auction, listing 1,220 players—939 Indians and 281 overseas cricketers from 17 countries—competing for a maximum of 66 available slots (including up to 25 overseas spots) after franchises retained or picked 184 players. The pool includes 184 capped players, 994 uncapped players (925 Indian, 127 with prior IPL experience), and 42 Associate-nation players led by the USA (13), UAE (10), and Nepal (10). Australia tops overseas entries with 53 players, ahead of South Africa (40) and West Indies (36). Franchises will now submit shortlists ahead of the final Auction List release on December 4, 2026.
Takeaway: Official auction pool breakdown setting up the December 16 IPL 2027 auction where RCB and CSK finalize their squads.
Motorsport.com: today · 3m
Max Verstappen voices discomfort over Middle East travel ahead of Qatar and Abu Dhabi  —  Arriving in Doha for the penultimate round of the 2026 Formula 1 season, four-time world champion Max Verstappen told Dutch daily De Telegraaf that he is uncomfortable finishing the 23-race calendar in Qatar and Abu Dhabi amid regional hostilities. Verstappen said he would rather stay home and noted it is unpleasant traveling where rockets can fly overhead in surrounding countries, though drivers are proceeding based on official safety guarantees. Earlier this year, F1 cancelled April's Saudi Arabian Grand Prix and relocated the Bahrain round to Malaysia's Sepang circuit in October—which Verstappen won from pole—before standing down Imola as a cold-weather backup for the finale.
Takeaway: Candid pre-race remarks from Max Verstappen as the 2026 F1 season enters its Qatar-Abu Dhabi closing double-header.
Formula 1: today · 3m
Pirelli sets 27-lap tyre stint cap for Qatar Grand Prix, mandating two pit stops  —  Formula 1 and Pirelli confirmed Wednesday that a mandatory 27-lap maximum stint limit per tyre set will apply across all sessions at this weekend's 2026 Qatar Grand Prix at Lusail International Circuit. Raised from last year's 25-lap cap, the precautionary restriction addresses high thermal stress and energy loads through Lusail's high-speed corners and forces at least two pit stops during the 57-lap night race. Pirelli is bringing its hardest compound range—C1 hard, C2 medium, and C3 soft—with cumulative lap counts including Safety Car and Virtual Safety Car laps.
Takeaway: Key technical and strategic constraint shaping qualifying and race strategy for this weekend's Qatar Grand Prix.

India chase first Test series win in New Zealand in 17 years ahead of Christchurch decider  —  India face New Zealand in the second and final Test at Hagley Oval in Christchurch starting Friday, seeking to end a 17-year wait for a Test series victory in New Zealand after the Wellington opener ended in a draw. Only 24 wickets fell across 407 overs on a flat Basin Reserve pitch where India piled up over 700 runs, highlighted by a KL Rahul century and Jasprit Bumrah dismissing New Zealand captain Tom Latham in the opening over of both innings. New Zealand have ruled out pacer Blair Tickner with a hamstring injury and recalled Matt Fisher while hoping Hagley Oval offers sharper seam assistance.
Takeaway: Pre-match team news and stakes for India's decisive second Test against New Zealand starting Friday.
beIN SPORTS: today · 3m
Lille stun depleted Bayern Munich 2-1 to hand German giants first loss of season  —  Bayern Munich suffered their first defeat in any competition this season on Wednesday, losing 2-1 away to Lille in UEFA Champions League Matchday 5. Missing Harry Kane to a serious ankle injury and Michael Olise to a foot complaint, Vincent Kompany fielded 18-year-old Lennart Karl as his lone recognised forward. Centre-back Dayot Upamecano headed Bayern in front on 15 minutes from a Tom Bischof corner, but Ayase Ueda equalised before an Aleksandar Pavlovic own goal gave Lille their first league-phase victory. The loss drops Bayern to 15th in the table with three matches left, while Lille move within two points of the knockout spots.
Takeaway: Headline upset from Wednesday's UEFA Champions League Matchday 5 slate.
