Nuvvula Guddu Porutu (Spiced Sesame Egg Scramble)నువ్వుల గుడ్డు పొరుటు

Fluffy scrambled eggs speckled with golden roasted sesame seeds, sharp green chillies, and fragrant curry leaves in hot ghee.

4.3g net carbs  ·  15g protein  ·  21g fat  ·  269 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 71% ● Protein 22% ● Net Carbs 6%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Kodiguddu Porutu / Egg Bhurji Andhra Style #cookwithchefmasaroor #eggbhurjirecipe #eggbhurji — Cook with Chef Masaroor
Ingredients
  • eggs, beaten 4 large eggs · 200g
  • white sesame seeds (nuvvulu), dry-roasted and coarsely crushed 1.5 tbsp · 15g
  • red onion, finely chopped 1/4 small onion · 40g
  • green chillies, slit lengthwise 2 small chillies · 10g
  • curry leaves (karivepaku), fresh 1 sprig · 3g
  • mustard seeds (aavalu) 1/2 tsp · 2g
  • cumin seeds (jeera) 1/2 tsp · 2g
  • turmeric powder (pasupu) 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • pure ghee 1 tbsp · 14g
  • table salt 1/2 tsp · 3g
  • fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped 1 tbsp · 5g
Method
  1. Dry-roast the sesame seeds in a small skillet over medium-low heat for 2 minutes until pale golden and popping; crush coarsely with a mortar and pestle or rolling pin and set aside.
  2. Melt ghee in a heavy kadai or iron skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds; as soon as the mustard crackles (about 30 seconds), add slit green chillies and curry leaves until they sizzle and stop spluttering.
  3. Add the finely chopped red onions with turmeric and salt; sauté over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes until soft, translucent, and lightly golden at the edges.
  4. Pour in the beaten eggs and lower heat to medium-low. Allow the base to set undisturbed for 20 seconds, then gently push cooked curds from edges toward the center with a spatula to form soft, moist folds.
  5. Sprinkle the crushed roasted sesame seeds and chopped coriander leaves over the soft curds; fold through for 30 seconds until the eggs are just cooked but still tender, then immediately remove from the heat to prevent drying out.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh Curry Leaves (Karivepaku): Frozen curry leaves (thawed and patted dry) or 1/2 tsp dried curry leaf powder
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara) or New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale / San Jose)
  • White Sesame Seeds (Tella Nuvvulu): Unhulled or hulled raw white sesame seeds from the standard spice/baking aisle
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Sprouts Farmers Market, Whole Foods Market, Trader Joe's, or Patel Brothers
Make it taste like home
  • Coarsely crushing the roasted sesame seeds rather than leaving them whole releases their essential oils into the eggs while keeping a satisfying texture.
  • Keep the heat gentle when folding the eggs; removing the pan while the eggs are slightly glistening ensures they stay juicy and velvety.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • To reduce heat from 4 to 2, deseed the green chillies or use half a mild Anaheim pepper instead.
  • For extra richness, substitute 7g of the ghee with unrefined cold-pressed sesame oil (nuvvula nune).

Allam Kodi Vepudu (Andhra Ginger Chicken Roast)అల్లం కోడి వేపుడు

Succulent chicken thighs seared in a pungent fresh ginger and green chilli paste, roasted dry with curry leaves and Guntur karam until darkly caramelized.

8.2g net carbs  ·  36g protein  ·  18g fat  ·  351 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 48% ● Protein 43% ● Net Carbs 10%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
ఘాటైన గుంటూరు చికెన్ రోస్ట్ రెసిపీ| Spicy Guntur Chicken Roast Recipe | Kodi Vepudu in Telugu — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized 2 cm pieces about 3 thighs · 350g
  • fresh ginger root, peeled and roughly chopped 2-inch piece · 25g
  • garlic cloves, peeled 4 cloves · 12g
  • green chillies (half ground in paste, half slit) 3-4 chillies · 12g
  • red onion, finely minced 1/3 medium onion · 50g
  • groundnut oil 1.5 tbsp · 20g
  • fresh curry leaves 2 sprigs · 4g
  • Guntur red chilli powder (karam) 1.5 tsp · 4g
  • coriander seed powder (dhaniyala podi) 1 tsp · 3g
  • turmeric powder (pasupu) 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • Andhra garam masala powder 1/2 tsp · 2g
  • black pepper, coarsely cracked 1/2 tsp · 2g
  • fresh lemon juice 2 tsp · 10g
  • salt 3/4 tsp · 4g
  • fresh coriander leaves, chopped 1.5 tbsp · 6g
Method
  1. Grind the fresh ginger, garlic cloves, and 2 green chillies in a small spice jar with 1 tablespoon of water into a coarse, pungent paste.
  2. Toss chicken thigh pieces with turmeric, half the salt (2g), and half the lemon juice (5g); let rest for 5 minutes while prepping the kadai.
  3. Heat groundnut oil in a wide heavy kadai over medium-high flame until shimmering. Add the remaining slit green chillies and curry leaves; fry for 20 seconds until crisp and intensely fragrant.
  4. Add minced red onion and sauté vigorously for 3 to 4 minutes until deep golden-brown. Stir in the freshly ground ginger-garlic-chilli paste and cook over medium heat for 2 minutes until the raw edge vanishes and oil glistens at the edges.
  5. Add the marinated chicken pieces to the hot pan; sear over medium-high heat for 4 minutes, turning occasionally until the meat turns opaque and releases its juices.
  6. Lower the heat to medium, cover the pan with a tight lid, and let the chicken braise in its natural juices for 5 minutes until fully tender.
  7. Uncover the pan, turn the heat to medium-high, and stir in Guntur chilli powder, coriander powder, cracked black pepper, and remaining salt. Sauté and roast continuously for 3 to 4 minutes until the moisture evaporates and the dark, fragrant allam masala clings tightly to every morsel of chicken.
  8. Dust with Andhra garam masala, drizzle the remaining lemon juice, and fold through fresh coriander; turn off heat and rest for 2 minutes before serving.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Guntur Karam (Fiery Andhra Red Chilli Powder): Equal parts ground cayenne pepper and Kashmiri chilli powder for balanced heat and vibrant color
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara) or New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale)
  • Fresh Curry Leaves (Karivepaku): Thawed frozen curry leaves from the Indian grocer freezer section, patted dry
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara) or Indian Market (Sunnyvale)
Make it taste like home
  • Using boneless chicken thighs instead of breasts prevents drying out during the high-heat roasting stage and ensures maximum juiciness.
  • Letting the chicken cook covered in its own steam first cooks the meat through quickly so you can roast it dry without burning the ginger.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • To reduce heat from 4 to 2, cut the chilli powder to 2g and omit the cracked black pepper.
  • Chicken breast can be substituted; reduce covered cooking time to 3 minutes to avoid overcooking.

Nellore Chepala Iguru (Coastal Andhra Fish Masala)నెల్లూరు చేపల ఇగురు

Firm fish fillets simmered gently in a concentrated, spicy tomato and roasted-fenugreek onion gravy until the sauce clings tight.

6.4g net carbs  ·  29g protein  ·  21g fat  ·  337 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 57% ● Protein 35% ● Net Carbs 8%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
పక్కా కొలతలతో అద్దిరిపోయే నెల్లూరు చేపల పులుసు | Nellore Chepala Pulusu @VismaiFood — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • catfish fillets or nuggets (or tilapia), cut into 4 cm pieces about 2 fillets · 350g
  • red onion, finely minced 1/3 medium onion · 50g
  • red ripe tomato, finely chopped 1/2 medium tomato · 50g
  • fresh ginger, finely grated 1 tsp · 8g
  • garlic cloves, finely grated or crushed 2 cloves · 8g
  • green chillies, slit lengthwise 2 chillies · 8g
  • unrefined sesame oil (nuvvula nune) or groundnut oil 1.2 tbsp · 18g
  • mustard seeds (aavalu) 1/2 tsp · 2g
  • cumin seeds (jeera) 1/2 tsp · 2g
  • fenugreek seeds (menthulu), dry-roasted and crushed 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • fresh curry leaves 1 sprig · 3g
  • Guntur red chilli powder (karam) 1.5 tsp · 4g
  • coriander seed powder (dhaniyala podi) 1 tsp · 3g
  • turmeric powder (pasupu) 1/4 tsp · 1g
  • fresh lemon juice 1 tsp · 5g
  • table salt 3/4 tsp · 4g
  • fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped 1.5 tbsp · 6g
Method
  1. Lightly coat catfish pieces with turmeric powder, half the salt (2g), and lemon juice; rest for 5 minutes on a plate.
  2. Heat sesame oil in a wide, heavy-bottomed skillet over medium heat. Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and crushed roasted fenugreek seeds; let the mustard crackle and fenugreek perfume the oil (about 20 seconds).
  3. Add curry leaves and slit green chillies, then immediately add minced red onion; sauté over medium heat for 4 minutes until golden brown.
  4. Add grated ginger and garlic; cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Add chopped tomato, Guntur chilli powder, coriander powder, and remaining salt. Sauté for 3 to 4 minutes, mashing tomatoes with the back of the spoon until soft and oil separates from the masala.
  5. Pour in 1/3 cup (80 ml) warm water, stir well, and bring the masala to a rapid simmer until it thickens into a glossy gravy base.
  6. Carefully lay the fish pieces in a single layer into the simmering gravy. Spoon the thick masala over the fish pieces. Cover skillet with lid and simmer on medium-low heat for 6 minutes.
  7. Uncover, gently swirl the pan by its handles (avoid using a spoon to prevent breaking delicate fish flakes), and simmer uncovered for 2 to 3 minutes until the gravy reduces to a luscious clingy coating (iguru).
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves, remove from flame, and let rest covered for 5 minutes before serving so the fish absorbs the deep masala flavors.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Freshwater River Fish / Murrel (Korameenu): Fresh US farmed channel catfish nuggets or firm tilapia fillets
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: 99 Ranch Market (San Jose / Cupertino), Safeway, or Whole Foods Market seafood counter
  • Gingelly / Cold-Pressed Sesame Oil (Nuvvula Nune): Unrefined Indian gingelly oil (Idhayam brand) or cold-pressed toasted sesame oil
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers (Santa Clara) or New India Bazaar (Sunnyvale)
Make it taste like home
  • Never stir fish curry vigorously with a metal ladle once simmering; always swirl the pan by the handles to keep delicate fish pieces completely intact.
  • A pinch of dry-roasted fenugreek powder is the secret soul of coastal Nellore fish gravies, giving a distinctive savory depth.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • To reduce heat from 4 to 2, cut chilli powder to 2g and discard green chilli seeds.
  • Tilapia, wild rockfish, or Atlantic salmon fillets work equally well in place of catfish.

iScience via PubMed: Oct 28 · 6m
Randomized crossover trial compares gut and immune markers on Mediterranean-ketogenic versus MCT-supplemented diets  —  In a randomized crossover trial published in iScience (NCT05469997), researchers evaluated 8 weeks of an energy-matched Mediterranean-ketogenic diet versus a Mediterranean diet supplemented with medium-chain triglycerides (MCTs), separated by an 8-week washout. Neither diet disrupted microbiome alpha- or beta-diversity, nor altered markers of gut barrier integrity (zonulin) or intestinal inflammation (calprotectin). While the carbohydrate-restricted ketogenic protocol modestly reduced total fecal short-chain fatty acids, both interventions significantly enriched the beneficial butyrate producer Roseburia hominis, and the MCT-supplemented arm reduced systemic inflammatory cytokines IL-1β and IL-8. The authors note the cohort was limited to Parkinson’s disease participants, warranting longer-term studies in wider populations.
Takeaway: Informs your keto gut-health and fat-quality strategies by showing that pairing low-carb eating or MCTs with fibrous vegetables preserves gut barrier integrity and beneficial Roseburia taxa.
Journal of Sports Sciences via PubMed: Oct 29 · 5m
Meta-analysis finds limited post-exercise recovery benefit from exogenous ketone supplementation  —  A systematic review and meta-analysis in the Journal of Sports Sciences evaluated 16 trials (7 meta-analyzed) assessing exogenous ketone supplements for athletic recovery in adults. Ketone ingestion showed no statistically significant effect on post-exercise muscle soreness (SMD = 0.39, p = 0.26) or muscle glycogen resynthesis rates (SMD = 18.21, p = 0.34). Post-exercise blood lactate showed a small difference favoring control conditions (MD = 0.34 mmol/L, p = 0.04), while creatine kinase levels were unaffected. Cognitive recovery was largely neutral aside from isolated reaction-time improvements. The authors emphasize that available literature is predominantly restricted to active young males, limiting broader generalizability.
Takeaway: Directly relevant to your workout recovery and supplement tracking, providing meta-analytic evidence that ketone supplements offer limited practical benefit for muscle soreness or glycogen recovery.
Nutrients via PubMed: Oct 20 · 6m
Narrative review examines how distinct ketone bodies modulate sleep architecture and slow-wave depth  —  A narrative review in Nutrients synthesizes mechanistic and clinical evidence examining how circulating ketone bodies influence sleep architecture and homeostasis. Preclinical investigations show that acetoacetate (AcAc), rather than β-hydroxybutyrate (BHB), enhances slow-wave delta power (0.5–4 Hz) during non-REM sleep and suppresses presynaptic glutamate release, identifying AcAc as an active neurochemical modulator. Human evidence remains heterogeneous: acute carbohydrate restriction often increases slow-wave sleep while suppressing REM duration, and ketone esters improve sleep efficiency primarily after late-evening strenuous exertion rather than during hypoxia. The authors stress that ketone concentration ranges for optimal sleep remain undefined, cautioning that ketones are conditional rather than universally sleep-promoting.
Takeaway: Connects your keto adaptation to sleep quality tracking, explaining how distinct ketone metabolites differentially alter non-REM slow-wave depth and REM duration.
Frontiers in Nutrition: Oct 12 · 6m
Frontiers meta-analysis highlights high glycemic heterogeneity and gastrointestinal side effects in diabetes keto trials  —  A meta-analysis of 25 randomized controlled trials in Frontiers in Nutrition compared Mediterranean, intermittent fasting, energy-restricted, and ketogenic diets in adults with type 2 diabetes. While all four dietary patterns yielded weight reduction (pooled mean -3.00 kg) and supported glycemic improvements, the ketogenic diet exhibited substantial inter-trial heterogeneity for fasting blood glucose (I² = 79.7%), limiting its universal clinical predictability. Crucially, none of the four dietary patterns demonstrated a statistically significant advantage in modifying lipid profiles (total cholesterol, LDL-C, or triglycerides). Safety analyses revealed that the ketogenic diet carried a higher incidence of transient gastrointestinal symptoms and mild hypoglycemia compared to the other patterns.
Takeaway: Provides critical trial context for your keto planning, showing that lipid responses vary substantially across randomized studies and that long-term adherence depends on managing digestive tolerance.
Metabolism via PubMed: 2 days ago · 5m
Metabolism review dissects the dual paradox of ketone utilization in cardiac energy stress  —  A review in Metabolism examines cardiac fuel switching in heart failure, where myocardial tissue upregulates ketone oxidation to compensate for impaired fatty acid and glucose metabolism. The authors outline a dual paradox: while circulating ketone bodies serve as an oxygen-efficient rescue substrate that preserves ATP generation under energetic strain, elevated fasting ketones also correlate with worsening hemodynamic severity and filling pressures. Furthermore, while the cardiovascular benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors are partly mediated by elevated ketone availability, this pathway carries clinical management trade-offs with euglycemic ketoacidosis. The review emphasizes distinct metabolic mechanisms across heart failure phenotypes that necessitate personalized metabolic therapeutics.
Takeaway: Adds foundational metabolic perspective to your keto reading, detailing how circulating ketones function as an energy-efficient rescue fuel for myocardial energetics under metabolic stress.
