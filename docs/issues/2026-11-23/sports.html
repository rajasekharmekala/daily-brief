Mercedes rival George Russell hit the barriers late while chasing and finished ninth, whereas Ferrari teammates Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc collided and took separate 10-second penalties to finish seventh and 10th. Newly crowned champion Kimi Antonelli finished fourth.

The stalemate leaves India fourth in the World Test Championship table as their points percentage slipped from 51.52 to 50, while New Zealand dropped from second (66.67%) behind South Africa (75%) and Australia (69.23%) before Christchurch.
