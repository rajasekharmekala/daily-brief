Putin agrees in principle to resume US-Russia-Ukraine talks after Nov. 10  —  After about three hours with US envoy Steve Witkoff in St. Petersburg, the Kremlin said Putin agreed in principle to resume trilateral talks with Kyiv and Washington after Nov. 10, focused on a post-war security system. Malay Mail: today · 3m
Iran says its naval mines halted three tankers in the Strait of Hormuz  —  Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on Oct. 20 that their naval mines stopped three tankers trying to transit Hormuz by routes Tehran calls illegal. The report fits a pattern of ship incidents under Iran's blockade and the US counter-blockade of Iranian ports, and keeps shipping and energy-market risk elevated. Claims come from the Guards; independent confirmation of damage varies by outlet.
Takeaway: Hormuz and Middle East energy risk are named interests; this is the latest shipping escalation.
The National: today · 3m
Libya's eastern parliament ratifies Turkey maritime deal; Greece, EU and Egypt object  —  Libya's eastern-based House of Representatives unanimously approved the 2019 Turkey-Libya maritime boundary agreement, reversing its earlier opposition and its decision to cut ties with Ankara. Greece called the deal illegal and said it will protect its sovereign rights; the EU leaders said they regret the vote. The pact bears on gas-rich claims in the eastern Mediterranean.
Takeaway: A geopolitics and energy story outside the headline wars, with trade-route and gas-exploration stakes.
The Straits Times (Reuters): today · 3m
Turkey readies troop mandate for Saudi Arabia under three-way defence pact  —  A ruling-party lawmaker told Reuters Turkey will ask parliament for a mandate to deploy military personnel to Saudi Arabia once the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement with Saudi Arabia and Pakistan is ratified. Initial deployments would be technical staff for air defence and electronic warfare, with no offensive role, as Saudi Arabia faces Houthi attacks.
Takeaway: Extends your Middle East thread to new alliance moves as Houthi attacks pressure the Gulf.
Connexion France: 2 days ago · 4m
France braces for more protests after weekend riots; yellow vests call new rallies Oct. 24  —  After 550,000 people marched across France on Oct. 17 per the interior ministry, riots and looting followed; over 2,000 arrests were reported. The prime minister asked prefects for localised curfews, and gilets jaunes groups are calling a second protest day on Oct. 24. Context: cost-of-living anger and fuel prices, with the 2027 election looming.
Takeaway: Broad world-affairs interest: Europe's political stability and fuel-price pressure tie into the energy shock from the Gulf.

India

Business Standard: today · 4m
RBI minutes: rate cuts off the table as members flag broadening inflation  —  Minutes of the RBI Monetary Policy Committee's 5-7 October meeting, released 21 October, show all six members backed a 25 bp repo hike to 5.50%, but Nagesh Kumar and Ram Singh opposed the stance change to calibrated tightening. The Governor said it signals rate cuts are unlikely near term. The MPC projects 5.2% inflation for 2026-27, touching 6% in the third quarter.
Takeaway: Broad interest: the RBI policy path for your India news follow-up.
India Today: today · 3m
Supreme Court holds Andhra Pradesh civic poll candidate list until 25 October  —  A Supreme Court vacation bench asked the Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission to defer publishing the final candidate list for about 100 urban local bodies until 25 October. It must consider YSRCP complaints that violence stopped nominations and decide by Thursday whether to extend the deadline. The DGP was told to provide security to candidates and election offices.
Takeaway: Andhra Pradesh politics is a stated focus of your India coverage.

Jaishankar on 20 October in Mumbai, his second India visit in under five months. Both sides welcomed progress on the bilateral trade agreement negotiations, and Rubio urged India to scale up energy trade and buy more American technology and systems, while ties remain strained over trade policy and tighter immigration rules.
Takeaway: Covers the US-India relations and visa-rules thread in your India coverage.

BRS says Godavari-Cauvery link from Polavaram threatens Telangana's water rights  —  BRS leader T. Chandrababu Naidu wrote to the Centre seeking the link from Polavaram and its inclusion on the agenda of the next National Water meeting. This is an opposition claim; the state government's view is not in this report.
