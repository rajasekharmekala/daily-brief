arXiv: Oct 9 · 10m
Do prompt optimizers break requirements they are not scored on? A preregistered study  —  A preregistered study runs GEPA, MIPROv2 and TextGrad on HotpotQA, SQuAD 2.0 and IFEval and counts violations of unscored requirements. On HotpotQA, JSON extraction failed for 0.7% of outputs with the initial prompt and 29.5% with ungated optimized prompts, which meets the preregistered regression criterion. A post-hoc analysis attributes this mainly to the 200-token output cap: optimized prompts reason before the JSON, so responses are cut off; among parseable outputs regression is within 3 points of zero, and a 1,024-token cap removes the extraction failures. Feeding violation feedback to the proposer cut new violations but cost 6-14 exact-match points for GEPA and TextGrad.
Takeaway: A cautionary result on measuring prompt optimizers: part of the apparent regression is an output-budget artifact, so check truncation before blaming the optimizer.
