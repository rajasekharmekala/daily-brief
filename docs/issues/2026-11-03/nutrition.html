Quick Gongura Guddu Kura (Eggs in Sorrel Curry)గోంగూర గుడ్డు కూర

Boiled eggs simmer in a sharp, leafy gongura paste with a quick garlic-and-curry-leaf tempering. A bold Andhra breakfast that needs no tiffin or bread.

5.6g net carbs  ·  14g protein  ·  16g fat  ·  232 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 65% ● Protein 25% ● Net Carbs 10%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Gongura egg curry Okasari ela cheyandi👌#gongurarecipes #gonguraegg#easyrecipe #telugurecipes #viral — VamikaAyuktha Reddy _vlogs
Ingredients
  • eggs, hard-boiled and peeled 4 large eggs · 200g
  • gongura leaves, chopped about 2 packed cups · 50g
  • onion, finely diced 20g
  • tomato, chopped 25g
  • garlic, chopped 6g
  • green chilli, slit 8g
  • ghee 10g
  • curry leaves 2g
  • mustard seeds 1g
  • cumin seeds 1g
  • turmeric 1g
  • red chilli powder 2g
  • garam masala 1g
  • salt 1g
Method
  1. Hard-boil the eggs, cool, peel and make two shallow slits in each.
  2. Warm half the ghee in a kadai. Add gongura, tomato, green chilli and half the onion; cover and cook on low until the leaves collapse and turn dark olive, about 6 minutes. Cool slightly and blend to a coarse-smooth paste.
  3. In the same pan heat remaining ghee. Add mustard and cumin; when they pop, add garlic and curry leaves and fry until fragrant, then add the remaining onion and cook until its edges turn golden.
  4. Lower heat. Stir in turmeric, chilli powder and salt for 10 seconds, then add the eggs and roll them through the masala for a minute. Add the gongura paste and 60 ml water; simmer uncovered 6–7 minutes until the sauce thickens and a little oil glistens at the edges. Finish with garam masala.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh gongura: Frozen chopped gongura if available; otherwise use French sorrel or baby spinach with a squeeze of lemon to restore tang.
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers in Santa Clara or New India Bazaar in Sunnyvale; French sorrel may be seasonal at Whole Foods or farmers markets.
Make it taste like home
  • Keep the gongura portion measured; its sourness carries the curry without tamarind.
  • For heat 2, use one small green chilli and 1 g chilli powder.
  • Cook the leafy paste a day ahead and refrigerate; reheat it fully before adding eggs.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Use lemon at the table if the leaves are mild; avoid adding tamarind.

Karivepaku Vellulli Royyala Vepudu (Curry-Leaf Garlic Prawns)కరివేపాకు వెల్లుల్లి రొయ్యల వేపుడు

Dry-fried prawns catch a dark, garlicky Guntur masala and plenty of curry leaf. The pan sauce stays clingy and bright with lemon, ready for a small side of cauliflower rice.

7.6g net carbs  ·  23g protein  ·  8g fat  ·  203 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 38% ● Protein 46% ● Net Carbs 16%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
Best Prawn Ghee Roast Recipe !! — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • shrimp, peeled and deveined, raw about 10 oz · 300g
  • onion, thinly sliced 25g
  • tomato, finely chopped 25g
  • garlic, crushed 10g
  • green chilli, slit 8g
  • curry leaves 4g
  • groundnut oil 12g
  • lemon juice 8g
  • turmeric 1g
  • red chilli powder 3g
  • coriander powder 2g
  • cumin seeds 1g
  • salt 1g
  • cauliflower, riced about 1 cup, optional to serve · 100g
Method
  1. Pat the prawns very dry. Toss with turmeric, half the chilli powder and a pinch of salt while the pan heats.
  2. Heat oil on medium-high. Add cumin, then curry leaves; when they stop spluttering, add garlic and green chilli and stir until the raw garlic smell fades.
  3. Add onion and fry 4 minutes until translucent with golden edges. Add tomato, coriander powder and remaining chilli; cook until tomato breaks down and the masala looks glossy, 3–4 minutes.
  4. Raise heat to high and add prawns in one layer. Toss for 2 minutes, then cover for 2 minutes. Uncover and stir just until opaque and curled, usually another 1–2 minutes; stop before they tighten. Finish with lemon.
  5. Optional: dry-fry the riced cauliflower in a separate pan with a teaspoon of ghee for 5 minutes until steam subsides; serve a small scoop under the prawns.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh curry leaves: Frozen curry leaves work well; if unavailable, finish with a small pinch of kasuri methi plus lemon, knowing the aroma differs.
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers, New India Bazaar, or the refrigerated herb case at 99 Ranch.
  • Fresh prawns: Use thawed frozen raw shrimp, patted fully dry before frying.
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Costco, 99 Ranch, Patel Brothers, or a local seafood counter.
Make it taste like home
  • Prawns go from tender to rubbery quickly; pull them as soon as they turn opaque.
  • For heat 2, cut the chilli powder to 1 g and remove the green chilli seeds.
  • The curry leaf-garlic base can be fried ahead; cook the prawns fresh.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Skip cauliflower rice to lower the meal’s carbs further; the dry fry is complete on its own.

Guntur Goat Keema and Brinjal Kuraగుంటూరు మటన్ కీమా వంకాయ కూర

Goat mince cooks down with browned onion, tomato and roasted spices while small brinjal pieces soften into the masala. Deep, peppery and spoonable without a rice-heavy gravy.

6.4g net carbs  ·  33g protein  ·  11g fat  ·  266 kcal  ·  Serves 2
● Fat 38% ● Protein 52% ● Net Carbs 10%
🎬 Video Walkthrough & Cooking Technique: Watch on YouTube →
GUNTUR SPECIAL GUTTI VANKAYA KARAM!!! — Vismai Food
Ingredients
  • goat meat, lean mince about 10.5 oz · 300g
  • eggplant, small dice about 1 small · 100g
  • onion, finely chopped 25g
  • tomato, finely chopped 25g
  • ginger, minced 5g
  • garlic, minced 8g
  • green chilli, slit 8g
  • groundnut oil 12g
  • curry leaves 2g
  • mustard seeds 1g
  • cumin seeds 2g
  • turmeric 1g
  • red chilli powder 3g
  • coriander powder 3g
  • garam masala 1g
  • salt 2g
  • coriander leaves, chopped 4g
Method
  1. Heat the kadai on medium-high and add oil, mustard and cumin. When mustard pops, add curry leaves, green chilli, onion and a pinch of salt. Fry 5–6 minutes, stirring, until the onion is deep gold at the edges.
  2. Stir in ginger and garlic for 30 seconds. Add goat mince; break it up and fry until it loses its pink colour and begins to brown, 5 minutes.
  3. Lower heat. Add turmeric, chilli and coriander powders; stir for 20 seconds so the powders bloom without scorching. Add tomato and 60 ml water, cover and simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Add diced eggplant, cover again and cook until the mince is done and the brinjal is soft but still holds shape, 6–8 minutes. If the pan dries before the eggplant softens, splash in water a tablespoon at a time.
  5. Remove the lid and cook 1–2 minutes until the oil separates at the edge. Stir in garam masala and coriander leaves; rest 3 minutes before serving.
🌉 US & Bay Area Ingredient Adaptations
  • Fresh goat mince: Ground lamb is the easiest supermarket stand-in; use the same weight and drain excess fat only if the pan becomes greasy.
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Halal meat markets in the South Bay or Patel Brothers/New India Bazaar butcher counters; ground lamb is common at Safeway, Sprouts and Whole Foods.
  • Small Indian brinjal: Use Japanese eggplant or globe eggplant cut into small dice.
    Where to buy in the Bay Area: Patel Brothers, New India Bazaar or 99 Ranch; globe eggplant is available at mainstream groceries.
Make it taste like home
  • Brown the mince before adding wet ingredients; this gives the quick cook a slow-cooked taste.
  • For heat 2, use 1 g chilli powder and one deseeded green chilli.
  • Cook the masala base ahead, chill promptly, and reheat until steaming before serving.
Keto Swaps & Variations
  • Add 60 g cauliflower rice as a side only if the day’s carb total allows.

eBioMedicine / PubMed: Oct 24 · 6m
Two-year CARBFUNC analysis finds mixed lipid changes on a saturated-fat-rich low-carb diet  —  A secondary analysis of the two-year CARBFUNC randomized trial assigned 193 adults with obesity to two higher-carbohydrate patterns or a low-carbohydrate, high-fat diet. The low-carb arm showed lower triglyceride-rich remnants and higher HDL-C and apoA1 than the higher-carbohydrate comparators, while LDL-C rose mainly in larger particles and apoB did not significantly increase. Interpretation is constrained by high attrition: 84% had left by 24 months, and adherence fell to 40–60% in the second year.
Takeaway: This is a timely, nuanced lipid finding for your ongoing interest in strict-keto food planning; the attrition and adherence limits matter as much as the favorable markers.
PubMed / Medical News: 7 days ago · 5m
A new trial explainer asks how keto liver findings translate beyond a controlled study  —  This recent Medical News explainer revisits a randomized comparison of ketogenic, Mediterranean and plant-forward low-fat diets after similar weight loss in adults with obesity and metabolic risk. Its framing is useful because the reported hepatic-metabolism differences come from a small controlled trial and should not be generalized automatically to every person or to long-term free-living eating. The summary and study record identify the paper’s scope, but no additional numerical claims are included here.
Takeaway: It gives a practical evidence-reading lens for keto: distinguish controlled-study signals from what a routine home menu can establish.
